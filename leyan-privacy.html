乐言输入法隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 4 月 25 日

概述

乐言输入法（以下简称“本应用”）是一款离线运行的中文拼音输入法。本政策说明 iOS 版本应用如何处理用户数据。

我们不收集任何数据

本应用在用户设备本地运行，不会：

  • 收集、记录或上传用户输入的内容，包括拼音、汉字和英文；
  • 收集设备标识、位置、联系人、通话记录、相册等个人信息；
  • 向网络发送用户数据。本应用不进行网络请求，iOS 键盘扩展也不要求开启“允许完全访问”（Full Access）。

本机存储

为实现输入法功能，本应用仅在设备本地保存以下数据，不会将其发送到设备之外：

  • 随应用提供的词库与语言模型文件；
  • 输入法设置项。

这些数据保存在 iOS 应用及其 App Group 容器中，卸载应用时会随之移除。

第三方组件

本应用不使用广告、分析或崩溃上报服务。

政策更新

如本政策有调整，更新后的版本将在本页面发布。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 25, 2026

Overview

Leyan Input Method (the “App”) is an offline Chinese pinyin input method. This policy describes how the iOS version of the App handles user data.

We collect no data

The App runs locally on your device. It does not:

  • Collect, log, or upload anything you type, including pinyin, Chinese characters, or English;
  • Collect personal information such as device identifiers, location, contacts, call logs, or photos;
  • Send user data over the network. The App makes no network requests, and its iOS keyboard extension does not require “Allow Full Access.”

Local storage

To provide input-method features, the App stores the following on your device only. It is not sent outside your device:

  • Dictionary and language-model files included with the App;
  • Input-method settings.

This data is stored in the iOS app and its App Group container and is removed when the App is uninstalled.

Third-party components

The App does not use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services.

Policy updates

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
