Privacy Policy

SmartLLM is designed to run supported language models locally on your device.

Data stored on your device

Chat conversations, downloaded models, and app settings are stored locally on your device. SmartLLM does not operate an account system and does not send local model conversations to the developer's servers.

Optional network features

If you enable or request web search, your search query is sent to DuckDuckGo to retrieve search results. Weather requests use Open-Meteo. These services may process requests under their own privacy policies.

Location

SmartLLM requests location access only when you use location or weather features. Location access can be denied in iOS Settings. When weather is requested for your current location, coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to retrieve the forecast. SmartLLM does not continuously track your location.

Images

Images selected for supported vision models are used locally for the conversation and are not sent to the developer. Model downloads are retrieved from the model hosting service selected by the app.

Analytics and advertising

SmartLLM does not use advertising or third-party analytics.

Contact

For support questions, please contact the developer through the App Store product page.
